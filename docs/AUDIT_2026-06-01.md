## 4. Recommended Core Thesis

Do not make the article's main claim "DQ wins when alpha < 1" for MOEX equities. The data do not contain that strict regime.

A defensible thesis is:

> For liquid MOEX equities, Diversification Quotient contains predictive information about future tail-event incidence beyond the classical Diversification Ratio. Conservative EVT estimation shows that ultra-heavy `alpha < 1` regimes are empirically rare in this sample, but portfolio-level DQ is strongly connected with tail-heaviness measures. This makes DQ useful as a tail-sensitive diversification diagnostic, while alpha-conditioned interaction models should be interpreted cautiously because of collinearity.

This is less flashy than the original hypothesis, but much more defensible.
